Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Faceless Studio is a personal desktop application used by a single person, its owner, to publish their own videos on their own YouTube channels. It has no other users and offers no public service. This policy explains what it does with the data Google gives it access to.

Data it accesses

Only after the owner signs in with Google and grants the permissions, and only for the channel they choose:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's finished videos, private, with their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time.
  • Read the channel (youtube.readonly): the channel's name and id, and the status and public counts (views, likes, comments, regional availability) of the videos it uploaded.
  • Read analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): the channel's YouTube Analytics reports, to see which videos perform well.

It does not access email, contacts, files, other Google services, other people's channels, or any data of viewers.

Where the data is stored

  • The permission (OAuth refresh token) is stored in the system keychain of the owner's computer.
  • The channel's name and id, upload settings and the records of uploaded videos are stored as files on the same computer.
  • Nothing is stored on, or sent to, any server other than Google's own APIs. There is no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party service in the application.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any machine-learning or AI model.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Faceless Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Faceless Studio uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnecting a channel in the application revokes its permission at Google and deletes the stored token.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the application's folder removes all the files it kept.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/LuisJavierRT/faceless-studio-app/issues.

Changes

If what the application does with Google data changes, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.
